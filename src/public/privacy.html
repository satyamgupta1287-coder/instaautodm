Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

AutoDM ("we", "our", "the app") is an Instagram automation tool that lets Instagram business/creator accounts automatically reply to comments and send direct messages based on rules you configure. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how you can request its deletion.

1. Information We Collect

  • Instagram account information: your Instagram user ID and username, obtained via Instagram's official OAuth login when you connect your account.
  • Access tokens: a long-lived access token issued by Instagram, used only to send messages and read comments on your behalf.
  • Comment data: the text, author username, and ID of comments on your posts, received via Instagram's Webhooks, so we can match them against your automation rules.
  • Automation logs: a record of which rule matched a comment and whether a DM was sent, for your own dashboard/history.

2. How We Use This Information

  • To detect comments on your Instagram posts that match rules you create.
  • To send automated direct messages or replies on your behalf, per your configured rules.
  • To show you activity logs and statistics inside your AutoDM dashboard.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your Instagram data for advertising.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share your Instagram data with third parties, except with Meta/Instagram's own APIs as required to operate the automation you configure.

4. Data Storage

Data is stored securely in our Firebase (Google Cloud) database, associated only with your authenticated account.

5. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time by:

  • Disconnecting your Instagram account from within the AutoDM dashboard, or
  • Emailing us at sgtechclub26@gmail.com with your Instagram username, and we will delete your stored access token, logs, and account data within 30 days.

6. Contact

For any privacy questions, contact us at sgtechclub26@gmail.com.
